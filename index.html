Privacy Policy

GEO Auditor — Chrome Extension

Last updated: March 2026  ·  Version: 1.0.0  ·  Contact: geo-auditor@proton.me

1. Overview

GEO Auditor is a free Chrome extension that analyzes the active web page to evaluate its visibility score in generative AI search platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, and others).

This policy describes what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

GEO Auditor does not collect personal data, does not track your browsing, and does not sell any data to third parties.

2. Data Collected

2.1 Data processed locally Stays on your device

  • Text content of the active web page (headings, paragraphs, JSON-LD schemas)
  • URL and domain of the active page
  • Results of previous audits (stored via chrome.storage.local)

2.2 Data sent to the publisher's proxy Ephemeral

To deliver certain features, the following data is transmitted to a Cloudflare Worker proxy operated by the publisher. This proxy relays requests to external APIs:

Data transmittedDestination APIPurpose
Domain only (e.g., example.com)OpenPageRank, SerpAPI, Reddit API, Wikipedia APIAuthority scoring and citation counting
Page text contentGroq APISemantic analysis of the page content

The proxy does not retain any data. All requests are ephemeral and non-logged.

2.3 Data NOT collected Never

  • Personal information (name, email, IP address)
  • Browsing history
  • Cookies or session data
  • Form data or credentials
  • User identifiers of any kind

3. Use of Data

Collected data is used exclusively to:

  • Calculate the GEO score of the analyzed page
  • Display optimization recommendations
  • Maintain a local audit history

Data is never sold, shared with third parties for commercial purposes, or used for advertising.

4. Storage & Security

  • Audit history is stored locally in your browser via chrome.storage.local
  • This data remains on your device and is not synchronized with external servers
  • You can delete the history at any time from the extension interface
  • All communications with the proxy are encrypted via HTTPS

5. Chrome Permissions

PermissionWhy it is needed
activeTab Read the URL and content of the active tab when the user triggers an audit
scripting Extract DOM content from the active page (headings, schemas, text)
storage Save audit history locally in the browser
sidePanel Display the audit panel alongside the page
<all_urls> Fetch /robots.txt and /llms.txt from any audited domain, and communicate with the Cloudflare Worker proxy. Required because the audited domain is chosen by the user at runtime.

7. Your Rights

In accordance with GDPR and applicable laws, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: view your audit history directly in the extension
  • Right to deletion: clear your history from the extension interface at any time
  • Right to object: uninstall the extension to stop all processing
